# Structural Anti-Patterns EN

## P-01 — Excessive Em Dash Use

**Sign**: Em dash (—) appears more than 3 times per 1000 words, or multiple times in one paragraph.  
**Problem**: AI overuses em dashes as a substitute for sentence restructuring.  
**Fix**: Replace with period, comma, or restructure. Keep em dashes only for genuine parenthetical punch.

---

## P-02 — Mandatory Rule of Three

**Sign**: Every argument comes in exactly three parts. Every list has exactly three items.  
**Problem**: AI defaults to tricolon as structure, not as rhetorical choice.  
**Fix**: Break the three. Use two if two is right. Use one if one hits harder.

---

## P-03 — Monotone Paragraph Length

**Sign**: Every paragraph is 3–4 sentences, ~60–80 words.  
**Problem**: Mechanical uniformity = AI fingerprint.  
**Fix**: Introduce a 1-sentence paragraph after a long one. Vary rhythm deliberately.

---

## P-04 — Symmetrical Paragraph Structure

**Sign**: Every paragraph: topic sentence → 2–3 supporting points → conclusion that restates the topic.  
**Problem**: Predictable structure collapses authorial voice.  
**Fix**: Start with an example. End without a conclusion. Break the frame.

---

## P-05 — False Parallelism

**Sign**: «From X to Y, from A to B» — forced parallel constructions not demanded by content.  
**Problem**: AI generates parallel form as a default, regardless of meaning.  
**Fix**: Only use parallelism when the content is genuinely parallel. Otherwise: vary syntax.

---

## P-06 — Abstract Opening

**Sign**: Chapter or section opens with a general statement («In today's rapidly evolving workplace...»).  
**Problem**: Violates the series principle — opening through scene / paradox / provocation.  
**Fix**: Open with a specific case, a sharp claim, or a provocative question.

---

## P-07 — Summarizing Conclusion Paragraph

**Sign**: Last paragraph begins with «In conclusion», «To summarize», «In summary», «Overall».  
**Problem**: The series voice does not summarize — it lands a punch or builds a bridge.  
**Fix**: Delete the summary. Replace with a closing hit or a bridge to the next chapter.

---

## P-08 — Excessive Boldface

**Sign**: Bold text appears more than 2–3 times per 500 words, or every paragraph has bold.  
**Problem**: AI marks its own «key points» — as if the reader can't judge what matters.  
**Fix**: Remove most bold. Keep only where truly needed for navigation or contrast.

---

## P-09 — Title Case Headings Overload

**Sign**: Every sentence or phrase in the text is capitalized as a heading.  
**Problem**: AI turns prose into outline fragments.  
**Fix**: Use headings sparingly. Prose carries the argument; headings only orient.

---

## P-10 — Hedge Stacking

**Sign**: «This could potentially suggest that it might be somewhat related to...»  
**Problem**: Multiple hedges in one sentence = author has no position.  
**Fix**: One qualifier maximum per claim. Author has a position — or explains why uncertainty is genuine.

---

## P-11 — Symmetric Argumentation

**Sign**: «On one hand... on the other hand...» without the author committing to a position.  
**Problem**: Series voice rule: author has a position. Symmetry violates the voice.  
**Fix**: Keep counterargument only if it's immediately dismantled. Otherwise: remove.

---

## P-12 — Coaching Register

**Sign**: «You might want to consider», «It would be helpful to», «Try to notice when».  
**Problem**: Author of the series states what is — doesn't offer choices.  
**Fix**: Remove soft suggestions. Restate as direct observation or analysis.

---

## P-13 — Hyphenation Inflation

**Sign**: «cross-functional», «data-driven», «solution-oriented», «results-focused» — one per paragraph.  
**Problem**: AI defaults to compound adjectives as a way to sound professional.  
**Fix**: Replace with direct phrasing or remove. «Driven by data» or «focused on results» — or delete entirely.

---

## P-14 — Returning to Said (Reformulation Bloat)

**Sign**: Last sentence of paragraph restates the first sentence in different words. No new information.  
**Problem**: Word count inflation. AI closes loops it opened.  
**Fix**: Delete the reformulation. If something is worth saying, say it once.
